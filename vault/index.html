Our Story

Curated Thrift Today.
Our Own Label Tomorrow.
Real Brands. Real Heat.

VAULT was born from a real obsession with street culture right here in Pakistan. We love iconic sneakers from legendary global brands, but we hated two things: insane retail hype markups, and fake knockoffs flooding the market.

Our solution? Curate authentic, original thrifted kicks. We hunt through global thrift bins, vintage archives, and collector lots to bring you 100% genuine grails at honest prices — cleaned, restored, and graded with absolute transparency.

This is just Chapter 1. Coming soon, we are expanding into curated vintage streetwear and thrifted apparel (tees, hoodies, archive jackets). And as our community grows, we will channel this archive DNA into our own manufactured label. Real heat, always.
